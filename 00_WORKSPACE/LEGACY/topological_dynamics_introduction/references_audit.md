# Audited reference corpus

Audit date: 2026-08-15

## Scope and method

This ledger accompanies `references.bib`. The corpus contains 102 works from the updated monitored-fermion sheet, 33 from the fermion-counting sheet, 15 works shared by both sheets, and 20 additional works from the prior introduction bibliography. Every record was checked against a publisher record and/or a versioned primary manuscript. Annotations were then checked independently for scope and rewritten to report only results supported by the cited source; project-specific interpretation is isolated in `projectrelevance`.

Deduplication was performed by DOI, versionless arXiv identifier, and normalized title. Published metadata supersedes preprint metadata while a corresponding arXiv identifier is retained. The 49 keys from the pre-replacement introduction bibliography are preserved.

## Material corrections

- The prior 141-work inventory counted `Leung2025` as introduction-only, but that exact DOI is already monitored-sheet Ref. 21. Deduplication therefore gives 140 unique works; retaining 141 would require duplicating one paper or adding a source outside the requested corpus.
- The BPJ author is encoded as `Bhuiyan, Asadullah`, yielding A. Bhuiyan rather than A. H. Bhuiyan.
- Gough--Guţă--James--Nurdin is recorded as a 2011 article in volume 11(3), pages 237--268, DOI 10.4310/CIS.2011.v11.n3.a3.
- The Starchl paper uses Mark H. Fischer, and Pöpperl's spelling is normalized.
- Klich's contribution is represented as an `@incollection` record with its book, editors, publisher, and page range.
- Abbreviated author lists were expanded, and preprint-only labels were replaced by published records whenever a publication could be verified.

## Validation results

- Structural validation found 140 unique keys and no duplicate DOI, versionless arXiv identifier, or normalized title.
- All 49 pre-replacement bibliography keys were preserved, and all 46 keys cited by `merged_introduction.tex` remain defined.
- Every entry has a nonempty source-supported `annotation`, provenance, verified status, audit basis, audit date, primary-source locator, and evidence locator; no entry contains `et al.`, a pending status, or the incorrect Bhuiyan name.
- DOI and versioned-arXiv metadata were resolved during the thematic audits and compared with the primary-source title and full author list; the exceptional 1993 Levitov--Lesovik article was checked against the official journal scan and record.
- The actual two-column standalone introduction and an isolated RevTeX `\nocite{*}` document exercising all 140 entries compile without undefined citations, BibTeX errors, or overfull boxes.
- `annotation`, `projectrelevance`, provenance, and audit fields are absent from the generated APS `.bbl`; they remain machine-readable only in the source bibliography.
