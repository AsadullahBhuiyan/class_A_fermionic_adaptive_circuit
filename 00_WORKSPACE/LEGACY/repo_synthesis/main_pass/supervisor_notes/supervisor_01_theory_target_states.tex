\begin{abstract}
This supervisor note synthesizes Workers 01--04 and an independent read-only audit of the exact domain-wall benchmark, flattened-Hamiltonian and domain-wall truncation analysis, overcomplete-Wannier form-factor truncation theory, perturbative expansion notes, and modular charge-spreading diagnostics.  The combined evidence supports a coherent target-state narrative: the exact domain-wall Chern-insulator ground state supplies a controlled chiral-edge benchmark; the overcomplete-Wannier (OW) flattened Hamiltonian supplies a practical Gaussian target covariance with robust edge entanglement already at one shell; finite OW truncation should be judged by the effective overlap form factor rather than by the Chern number of the compact-support spinor itself; and modular spectra show striking agreement between exact and OW states while spatial modular dynamics remain eigenvector-sensitive.  The main adjustments are that domain-wall-sector truncation is a physically active hard boundary rather than a passive numerical improvement, that OW one-shell entanglement fidelity is stronger than OW one-shell Chern quantization, and that perturbative projection results justify local contraction intuition but not exact global convergence to the flattened-Hamiltonian target.
\end{abstract}

\section{Synthesis of Worker Claims}

\subsection{Exact domain-wall benchmark}

Worker 01's central claim is well supported.  The exact benchmark realizes a two-orbital lattice Dirac-Chern model
\begin{equation}
h(\bm{k})=\sin k_x\,\sigma_x+\sin k_y\,\sigma_y+
(\alpha-\cos k_x-\cos k_y)\sigma_z ,
\end{equation}
with a spatial mass slab and two domain walls.  The exact Gaussian state is obtained by diagonalizing the one-body Hamiltonian, occupying negative-energy modes, and forming
\begin{equation}
G=2P_- - \Id .
\end{equation}
The fixed-wall squared correlator follows the chiral-fermion expectation
\begin{equation}
|\langle \psi^\dagger(y)\psi(0)\rangle|^2\sim
\sin^{-2}(\pi y/L),
\end{equation}
with reported wall slopes near $-2.025$ and excellent $R^2$.  The integrated entanglement contour gives a full-width log-chord slope near $0.344$ and wall-window slopes near $0.169$, consistent with two additive chiral wall contributions.

The most important procedural point is that the contour subsystem is the full-width strip in $x$; the later $x$ window is only a diagnostic restriction on the already-computed contour.  The Boss should preserve this distinction because changing the subsystem would change the entanglement problem.

\subsection{Flattened Hamiltonian and DW truncation}

Worker 02 correctly identifies the OW flattened Hamiltonian as a signed sum of rank-one projected Wannier modes,
\begin{equation}
\Hflat=
\sum_{\bm R}\left(P_{A+}(\bm R)+P_{B+}(\bm R)
-P_{A-}(\bm R)-P_{B-}(\bm R)\right),
\end{equation}
with target covariance
\begin{equation}
\GO=2P_{\rm occ}-\Id .
\end{equation}
The audit confirms the hierarchy: at $N_x\times N_y=20\times 40$, $\alpha_1=1$, $\alpha_2=30$, one-shell OW states reproduce edge entanglement slopes very accurately, while Chern quantization is much more sensitive to support and sector leakage.  In the small CSV summary, no-truncation full-support variants sit near $\Chern\simeq0.796$, whereas full-support DW-truncated variants reach $\Chern=0.99999895$.

The adjustment is conceptual: DW-sector truncation is not merely a better cutoff.  The source explicitly treats it as a hard OW-function boundary at the domain-wall interfaces.  It can expose edge channels even when both sectors are topological, and in the physical $\alpha_2$ sweep it counts the loss of flank-sector chiral channels as $\alpha_2$ crosses the topological transition.

\subsection{OW form-factor truncation and perturbative expansion}

Worker 03's distinction is essential for the final theory narrative.  A finite-support OW spinor is not itself the object whose Chern number should be equated with the target band topology.  The relevant scalar is the effective overlap form factor
\begin{equation}
f^{(g)}(\bm{k}') =
\frac{1}{N}\sum_{\bm{k}}
\tilde g(\bm{k}'-\bm{k})e^{-i(\bm{k}'-\bm{k})\cdot\bm R}
f(\bm{k})\langle u_{\bm{k}'}|u_{\bm{k}}\rangle ,
\end{equation}
whose zero and winding encode the obstruction seen by the truncated OW measurement channel.  For the $\alpha=1$, $X$-trial case, the $n_{\rm shell}=1$ square mask retains $0.992155$ of the untruncated OW norm and preserves the overlap zero, displaced to $k_x/\pi\simeq0.4922$ from the unwindowed $0.5$.

The perturbative result should be used locally.  Around a fixed branch satisfying $G_*P=PG_*=sP$, a rank-one update linearizes to
\begin{equation}
\delta G'=(\Id-P)\delta G(\Id-P),
\end{equation}
and a raster cycle composes these projectors as $\delta G\mapsto L\delta G L^\dagger$.  This is strong evidence for contractive projection intuition, but it is not by itself a proof that the full nonlinear circuit converges exactly to $\GO$.

\subsection{Modular charge spreading}

Worker 04's modular summary is also supported.  The modular diagnostic compares the exact DW ground state with eight flattened-$\sigma_x$ OW surrogates on a $20\times40$ torus.  The half-system covariance is averaged over translated half-cuts before constructing
\begin{equation}
h_{\rm mod}=-2V\,\operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{arctanh}g_i)\,V^\dagger .
\end{equation}
The small spectrum CSV records identical modular ranges for all nine states:
\begin{equation}
\varepsilon_{\min}=-23.718998027710036,\qquad
\varepsilon_{\max}=+23.718998027710036,
\end{equation}
with $800$ modes and zero Hermiticity error.

The important caveat is that spectral equality does not imply eigenvector equality.  Standard OW surrogates qualitatively reproduce chiral, wall-guided spreading but leak more charge into the trivial region than the exact state.  DW-truncated OW variants impose exact confinement by construction.

\section{Independent Repo-Audit Adjustments}

First, the notation for $\alpha_1,\alpha_2$ is not uniform across source areas.  The exact benchmark note uses $\alpha_{\rm triv}\equiv\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_{\rm topo}\equiv\alpha_2$ with default values $(30,1)$, whereas the flattened-Hamiltonian and modular analyses use $\alpha_1=1$ for the topological slab and $\alpha_2=30$ for the trivial flanks.  The physics is consistent, but the Boss should avoid merging these conventions without an explicit translation.

Second, the strongest robust target-state claim is entanglement-structural, not full topological quantization at smallest shell.  One-shell OW gives wall-window slopes near $1/6$ and full-width slopes near $1/3$, but its Chern diagnostic remains near $0.795$ rather than $1$.  The source itself interprets this as entanglement being less sensitive to finite OW support than the global Chern marker.

Third, DW truncation should be described as a boundary condition in OW-function space.  It improves full-support Chern quantization and enforces modular confinement, but it can also change eigenvectors relative to the exact DW ground state.  Thus it is excellent for isolating topological-region dynamics, but not always the literal spatial response of the exact Hamiltonian ground state.

Fourth, the post-selected circuit comparison is approximate.  The CPU metadata records the canonical entry point \texttt{classA\_U1FGTN.run\_markov\_circuit}.  The normalized Frobenius distance to $\GO$ drops rapidly but saturates near $0.753$ without DW truncation and $0.721$ with DW truncation at 40 cycles.  This should be framed as approach to the OW target up to a finite fixed-point offset, not exact preparation.

Fifth, the modular spectrum equality is striking enough to preserve, but it should be presented with its construction attached: $y$-averaged half-cylinder covariance, large trivial mass, and flattened-$\sigma_x$ OW variants.  Spatial modular observables remain discriminating even when spectra coincide.

\section{Contradictions and Caveats}

The main convention caveat is the swapped use of $\alpha_1,\alpha_2$ between the exact benchmark and later OW/modular analyses.  The Boss should refer to ``topological mass'' and ``trivial mass'' when synthesizing across folders, or explicitly state the local convention.

The modular note contains a covariance-language tension: it describes a Majorana-like anti-Hermitian convention in prose but then uses the complex-fermion relation $C=(\Id+G)/2$ and a Hermitian spectral construction.  The numerical summaries report Hermitian modular kernels.  The final narrative should emphasize the operational formula rather than overstate a convention.

The exact equality of all nine modular spectra is source-supported, but surprisingly strong.  Since I did not reload arrays or rerun notebooks, this should be treated as a recorded repository result.  It is safe to preserve, but worth scrutinizing if the Boss wants an independently regenerated flagship claim.

The post-selected Frobenius comparison does not establish exact convergence to the OW flattened-Hamiltonian ground state.  It establishes rapid approach followed by a nonzero offset in the inspected finite-size, one-shell runs.

The finite-shell form-factor conclusion is strongest for the documented $\alpha=1$, $X$-trial, square-mask case.  Other trial orbitals, gauges, disorder, or DW-sector masks may require their own effective-form-factor check.

\section{Cross-Supervisor Addendum}

The five supervisor notes agree on the basic separation of objects.  The exact DW and OW flattened-Hamiltonian states define target-state and benchmark structure, while the production adaptive circuit is a trajectory-resolved Born-sampled Markov process.  Perfect correction is not postselection: it samples Born outcomes and then applies deterministic correction after mismatches. The robust evidence layer is the modern manifest/run-summary layer with canonical entry points, actual sample counts, observer policy, and source identity.
