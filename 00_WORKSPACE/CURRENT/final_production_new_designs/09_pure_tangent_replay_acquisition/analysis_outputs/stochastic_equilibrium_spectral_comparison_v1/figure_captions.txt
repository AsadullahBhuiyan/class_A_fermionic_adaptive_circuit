COMMON PRODUCTION CONTRACT. Nx=20, hard/support-truncated walls x=5,15; alpha_1=1, alpha_2=30, nshell=1, trial orbital X, zero twists. Pure initialization with an inert product exterior; slab-only perfect correction, raster_y, endpoint at 2Ny cycles. Each size has S=100 independent trajectories. Equilibrium is one deterministic half-filled ground state of the OW parent at matching geometry. Matrix convention Q_A=2G_A-I with G the occupation matrix. Subsystem A contains all x, both orbitals and Ny/2 consecutive y rows. Error bars/bands described as SEM use independent trajectories, never eigenvalues, translated cuts, or time slices. Origins are averaged within trajectories before ensemble statistics. All figures are vector PDFs and 300-dpi PNGs.

matched_normalized_densities: Nx=20, Ny=32, cycle 64, stochastic S=100 and deterministic equilibrium S=1. The original y0=0 cut and translated y0=8 cut use the same samples. Panel (a) has 50 common equal-width bins in [-1,1], retaining abs(lambda)<1-1e-8 and normalizing each protocol to unit integrated retained-mode density. Panel (b) displays epsilon=-2atanh(lambda) on [-6,6] in 48 bins, keeping the full retained-mode normalization rather than renormalizing the displayed window. Vertical axes are logarithmic; empty bins remain gaps. Shading is a pointwise trajectory SEM from the ratio estimator's influence function. No fit or smoothing kernel is used.

spectral_shape_and_level_splitting: Same 20x32 samples and endpoint. Panel (a) counts modes per state with abs(lambda)<a, without histogram bins or tail-dependent density normalization. One-parameter zero-intercept fits compare a and atanh(a), using 181 thresholds a=0.05...0.95; the fits are descriptive, not tests treating thresholds as independent observations. Panel (b) orders the lowest 24 absolute entanglement energies at y0=8. Stochastic markers are the mean, shading is the 10--90% trajectory range, and ten faint individual trajectories are shown. The equilibrium curve has no sampling uncertainty. The plotted degeneracies refer to finite-size levels and do not define an asymptotic spectral law.

cut_origin_and_spatial_excess: S=100, 20x32, cycle 64. Panel (a) evaluates all 16 inequivalent half-strip origins in every trajectory; complementary cuts have equal entropy. Each point's error bar is trajectory SEM. The gray reference is the average over origins and trajectories; green is equilibrium. Panel (b) shows the additive Gaussian entropy contour integrated over subsystem y and orbitals at each x, subtracting the matching equilibrium contour. It is not the entropy of a separate x subsystem. Error bars are trajectory SEM. Vertical references mark wall columns x=5,15; no fit is used.

size_scaling_and_entropy_variance: Nx=20, Ny=24,28,32,40,50,60; S=100 per fixed-origin point at cycle 2Ny. Origin averages use S=100 at Ny=32 and S=20 at Ny=40,50,60, one predetermined trajectory from every immutable five-sample shard; cuts are averaged first. Panel (a) error bars are trajectory SEM. The associated report fits fixed-origin S=b+m*log(Ny) over all six sizes, with inverse-SEM-squared weights; no fit curve is drawn here. Panel (b) is the ratio of mean entropy to mean intrinsic charge variance, not the mean of sample ratios. Reference values 3 and pi^2/3 come from ideal constant covariance and entanglement-energy densities over their complete supports.

saved_partial_postselection_control: Legacy within-campaign comparison at Nx=20, Ny=40, hard walls, nshell=1, alpha_1=1, alpha_2=30, default pure initialization, raster_y, 40 cycles. S=10 trajectories for p<1, and one deterministic trajectory for full postselection p=1. Origin-averaged half-strip entropies are used. Panel (a) first averages cycles 31--40 inside each trajectory, then reports trajectory mean and SEM; the deterministic point has no sampling uncertainty. Panel (b) shows cycle-resolved ensemble means for p=0,0.5,1 over cycles 5--40. Protocol/preparation differences prevent pooling with the primary production endpoints. No fit is used.

ordered_protocol_control: Nx=20, Ny=32; stochastic S=100 at cycle 64 versus one deterministic target-outcome trajectory at cycle 32. The latter starts from production sample 0's active state, with the inert exterior factored out and restored for observables, and has fixed active rank 362. Only invariant-checked checkpoints through cycle 32 are used; this is not a stationary-state claim. Panel (a) subtracts each protocol's own origin mean to compare the cut-position dependence. Stochastic errors are trajectory SEM; the postselected line has no sampling uncertainty. Panel (b) subtracts the minimum active-region OW-parent energy at each state's own active charge. Stochastic mean and SEM are compared with the single deterministic point. Exterior energy is excluded. Different final charges and cycle counts preclude interpreting this as a noise-only causal effect. No fit is used.
