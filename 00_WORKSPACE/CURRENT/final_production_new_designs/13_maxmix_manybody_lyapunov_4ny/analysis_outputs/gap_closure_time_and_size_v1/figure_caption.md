Finite-time Lyapunov-gap scaling for maximally mixed active-slab initialization at hard domain walls. The ensemble contains 100 independent Born trajectories at each Ny=20,24,30,36,44,56,60, with Nx=20, walls x=5,15, alpha1=1, alpha2=30 and nshell=1, evolved through T=4Ny. For each trajectory, the minimum absolute occupation logit is divided by 2T before averaging. (a) Ny times the mean finite-time gap versus T/Ny; shaded bands show one trajectory SEM. (b) Means at T/Ny=2,3,4 fitted independently to d0+a/Ny using all seven sizes, with d0 unconstrained. Data error bars show one SEM. Fit ribbons and intercept error bars show pointwise 95% confidence intervals from 5000 whole-trajectory bootstrap samples. Temporal correlations are retained by resampling each trajectory jointly across times; weights are fixed at the original inverse-SEM-squared values. Gray shading marks the unmeasured size range; dashed fit extensions are extrapolations. The intercept is a fixed-aspect-ratio extrapolation, not a demonstrated time-asymptotic gap. Supplementary sensitivity results compare omission of small sizes and addition of b/Ny^2.
