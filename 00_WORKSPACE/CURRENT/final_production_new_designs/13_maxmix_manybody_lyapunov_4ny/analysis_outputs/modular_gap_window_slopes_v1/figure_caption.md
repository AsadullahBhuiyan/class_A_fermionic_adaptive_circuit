Finite-window purification rates extracted from raw modular-gap growth. Nx=20 with hard walls x=5,15, alpha1=1, alpha2=30 and nshell=1; 100 independent Born trajectories per Ny=20,24,30,36,44,56,60, initialized maximally mixed in the active slab after exterior preparation and evolved through T=4Ny. The instantaneous raw modular gap is the minimum absolute occupation logit within each trajectory. For every trajectory, fit g_s(T)=2 Delta_hat_s T+b_s by ordinary least squares with a free intercept, separately over the inclusive windows T/Ny=[1,2],[2,3],[3,4], before averaging the fitted rates. (a) Mean rate versus circumference. (b) Circumference times mean rate. Error bars are one SEM across 100 trajectory-wise fits; lines connect data. All trajectories are retained, including negative noisy finite-window slopes. Paired 95% whole-trajectory bootstrap intervals for the difference between the last two windows include zero at every size. This tests window stability, not a proof of convergence at arbitrarily late time. Supplemental panels show raw-gap means and average fitted lines at Ny=20,36,60.
