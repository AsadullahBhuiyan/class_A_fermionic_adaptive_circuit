# Postselected alpha1=1 spatial purification

Caption: Column-resolved entropy s_x(t)=sum_y s(x,y,t) at x=5,10,15,
for (a) hard/support-truncated and (b) soft/untruncated walls. Nx=20,
Ny=40, alpha1=1, alpha2=30, nshell=1, raster-y ordering, full postselection,
no feedback, and maximally mixed initialization. One trajectory per wall
construction; hard-wall exterior modes are fixed product states. All saved
cycles 0..160 are shown. Entropy includes both orbitals and is in nats,
without division by Ny. Linear cycle and logarithmic entropy axes.
There is no averaging, fitting, smoothing, or uncertainty band.

Original v2 result/completion pairs are verified against DOWNLOAD_MANIFEST.json
and completion hashes. Per-cell contours are summed over y and checked against
the stored column contour and total entropy. Curves and provenance are saved
as curves.csv and summary.json. The hard-wall x=5 and x=15 curves nearly overlap.
Only selected columns are shown; they need not sum to the total entropy.
