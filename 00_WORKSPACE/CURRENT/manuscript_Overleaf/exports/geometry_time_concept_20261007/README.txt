Standalone domain-wall geometry with time slices

Three diagonally offset copies of the lattice and slab geometry. Earlier slices
use translucent blue-gray fills; the final foreground slice carries the original
OW supports, phase labels, periodic seam markings, and spatial dimension arrows.
The diagonal arrow denotes increasing circuit cycle t.

The background slices are schematic geometry copies, not simulation snapshots.
The manuscript source, PDF, figure bundle, fits, and data are unchanged.

Reproduce from this checkout:
  python exports/geometry_time_concept_20261007/draw_time_stack.py

Outputs: vector PDF and 300-dpi PNG, 7.05 x 7.5 inches, embedded CM/AMS fonts.

Visibility revision: background-layer opacities are 0.72 and 0.86, with darker
lattice points and boundaries. Phase, occupation, and time labels are 19 pt;
dimension labels are 17 pt. The previous version is retained in previous_v1/.

Phase labels now sit inside the foreground regions, aligned at y=26.5,
without background boxes.

Both earlier layers carry matching single top/bottom seam marks and double
left/right seam marks, with hidden edges occluded by later layers.

Background seam marks match the outline color and opacity of their layer.
Both dimension arrows and the cycle arrow use a 1.2 pt stroke.
