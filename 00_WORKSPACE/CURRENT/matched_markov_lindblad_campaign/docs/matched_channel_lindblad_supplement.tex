\section{Notation and local-map derivation}

This supplement uses the overcomplete Wannier (OW) conventions of
Bhuiyan--Pan--Jian.  For a normalized OW state
$|\widetilde W_{\boldsymbol r,\nu,n}\rangle$, set
$P=|\widetilde W\rangle\langle\widetilde W|$ and
$\hat N=\hat\chi^\dagger\hat\chi$.  The number-dephasing adjoint action closes on the
two-point correlation matrix:
\begin{equation}
 \Diss^\dagger[\hat N](G)=PGP-\frac12\{P,G\}.
\end{equation}
Unit perfect filling and depletion contribute, respectively,
\begin{equation}
 P-\frac12\{P,G\},\qquad -\frac12\{P,G\}.
\end{equation}
Writing $\mathcal I$ for the identity superoperator, adding the number-dephasing term
gives the exact filled and empty reset identities
\begin{align}
 \mathcal R_{P,-}-\mathcal I
 &=\Diss^\dagger[\hat\chi^\dagger]+\Diss^\dagger[\hat N],\\
 \mathcal R_{P,+}-\mathcal I
 &=\Diss^\dagger[\hat\chi]+\Diss^\dagger[\hat N].
\end{align}
Thus each exact reset has the form $\mathcal R_a=\mathcal I+\mathcal L_a$.
The canonical within-cell order is $A+$ (loss), $A-$ (gain), $B+$ (loss), and
$B-$ (gain).  By contrast, $\mathcal I+\Diss^\dagger[\hat\chi]$ and
$\mathcal I+\Diss^\dagger[\hat\chi^\dagger]$ at unit coefficient are formal Euler
increments, not exact completely positive reset maps.  Expanding a product of weakened
reset maps $\mathcal R_{P}(p)=\mathcal I+p[\mathcal R_P-\mathcal I]$ gives
\begin{equation}
 \prod_a\mathcal R_{P_a}(p)=\mathcal I
 +p\sum_a[\mathcal R_{P_a}-\mathcal I]+O(p^2),
\end{equation}
which fixes the continuous generator and shows why its first derivative is independent
of site order even though the finite product is not.
For the physical unit-step sweep,
$\mathcal E_{\boldsymbol s}=\mathcal R_M\cdots\mathcal R_1
=\mathcal I+\mathcal L_{{\rm cycle},\boldsymbol s}$ is also an exact identity, but
$\mathcal L_{{\rm cycle},\boldsymbol s}=\mathcal E_{\boldsymbol s}-\mathcal I$
contains schedule-ordered cross terms.  The matched infinitesimal generator is instead
$\sum_a\mathcal L_a$ (or the corresponding dephasing-deleted sum).

\section{Translation twirl and global momentum weights}

The spatial sliding estimator is the finite-group conditional expectation
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal T_y[G]=\frac1{N_y}\sum_{q=0}^{N_y-1}T_y^qGT_y^{-q}.
\end{equation}
It is Hermiticity preserving, trace preserving, positive, idempotent, and exactly
translation invariant.  These properties are tested directly.  They do not imply that
the untwirled ordered channel commutes with $T_y$.

For global eigenmodes, Parseval normalization gives
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{k_y}Z_j(k_y)=
 \sum_{x,y,\mu}|\phi_j(x,y,\mu)|^2=1.
\end{equation}
The archive retains both the global occupations and $Z_j(k_y)$ so that a
momentum-weighted spectral function can be reconstructed without assuming symmetry.

\section{Array-level storage contract}

Every cycle-resolved observable has leading logical axes $(s,c)$, with exact sample IDs
and $c=0,1,\ldots,2N_y$.  Only inexpensive traces, norms, and translation diagnostics
are evaluated at every cycle.  Occupation spectra and entropy proxies are evaluated at
the explicit checkpoint cycle list in the configuration.  Final and late-average dense
correlation matrices are checkpoint-resolved and retain their sample axis.

The response product retains
\texttt{response\_density\_source\_wall\_time\_y} with logical shape
$(s,y_0,a,t,y)$ before source averaging.  The derived source-mean profile has shape
$(s,a,t,y)$.  Channel and continuous time coordinates are saved explicitly and are not
silently treated as identical grids.

Each completed case directory contains exactly one compressed observable file, one JSON
metadata file, and a success marker.  A failed case has a failure record and failure
marker.  Temporary directories are renamed atomically; existing case and run roots are
never overwritten.  The run manifest pins configuration, solver, adapter, canonical
class, reference-code, and git-state hashes.

\section{Legacy reconciliation}

The prior stationary solver used dephasing-free gain and loss with equal coefficient
$1/2$.  The new dephasing-free perfect-correction continuous generator has equal unit
coefficients.  Hence
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal L_{\rm old}=\frac12\mathcal L_{\rm PC}^{(0)},
 \qquad G_{\rm ss,old}=G_{\rm ss,PC}^{(0)}
\end{equation}
when the geometry and OW projectors match.  State-only stationary estimators can be used
as cross-checks.  Relaxation rates, fixed-time response, and the old auxiliary Strang
map cannot be relabeled as results of the requested dynamics.

\section{Claim discipline}

A channel twirl is not an exact symmetry of a fixed ordered sweep.  A natural-occupation
crossing is not a zero Liouvillian decay rate.  A signed density-lobe velocity is not a
Hamiltonian group velocity or quantized Hall coefficient.  Ten schedules quantify only
the specified schedule ensemble at the canonical endpoint.  These distinctions remain
in captions, tables, and machine-readable analysis metadata.
