Normalized full-system entropy contour at cycle 60 for alpha1=1, Nx=20, Ny=30, S=100 independent full-measurement trajectories starting from a fully maximally mixed state. Each saved contour s_m(x,y) sums to that trajectory's full-system entropy S_m. We divide each map by S_m and then average, so the displayed map sums to one and each trajectory has equal weight. Both orbitals are summed; y dependence is retained with no translation average, smoothing, or interpolation. This is the full-system purification entropy contour, not the half-strip entanglement contour. No new simulations or manuscript changes.
