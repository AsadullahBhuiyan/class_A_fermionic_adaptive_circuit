Standalone eigenmode-position analysis, 2026-10-08

Input: saved cycle-60 full-system maximally mixed alpha1=1 ensemble underlying Figure 3(b), Nx=20, Ny=30, S=100. No new simulations and no manuscript edits.
The saved centered covariance is G_final=2C-I. We diagonalize Hermitian C, preserving occupations at roundoff accuracy relative to every saved spectrum. Matrix coordinate order is (y,x,orbital).
For each normalized eigenmode j, p_j(x)=sum_(y,orbital)|u_j(y,x,orbital)|^2, <x>_j=sum_x x p_j(x), and sigma_x=sqrt(<x^2>-<x>^2).
Figure (a): all 120,000 modes pooled without rank averaging; substantially mixed modes, 0.005<nu<0.995, highlighted. Figure (b): mean sum_j p_j(x) indicator(nu_j in bin), with 40 uniform occupation bins and logarithmic colors. This spatial spectral-projector trace avoids an artificial single center for modes supported on both walls and arbitrary rotations of numerically degenerate near-pure eigenvectors. No smoothing or pseudocounts.
The linear coordinate has the x=0..19 torus cut; individual centers are not invariant under coordinate-cut changes or rotations in degenerate eigenspaces. The map is invariant under rotations within eigenspaces inside each occupation bin.
Result: 123 substantially mixed modes across 100 trajectories, average 1.23, range 0..2. Mean probability in columns x=5,6,14,15 is 0.9585638251. Eight modes have >10% probability on each wall. This is a finite-time diagnostic, not a count of topologically protected modes.
