# Endpoint correlator summary: tail-window revision

Generated by `../../build_correlator_summary_3x1.py`. The previous v1 outputs
remain unchanged. The report uses this revision's three-panel figure.

The primary collapse uses all six primary alpha1=1 ensembles, Ny=24,28,32,40,50,60,
with 100 trajectories per size. It fits 8 <= ry <= Ny/2, with a measured antipodal
anchor and equal total weight per size. The unconstrained exponent is
2.1903330477862717. The mean is formed before the logarithm; this is not a mean
of trajectory-wise exponents. Light gray denotes the union of fit ranges in
the normalized chord coordinate and darker gray their intersection. All points
retain their system-size color and open marker, inside and outside the fit window;
the legend is unchanged. Fit membership is still recorded in the plotted-data table.

The PDF and 300-dpi PNG are mirrored into the technical report's figures folder.
`summary.json` records verified inputs and source hashes. `compact_curves.npz`
contains unmasked correlators; `plotted_data.csv` identifies displayed/fitted
points. The additional plots and window table are diagnostics, not report figures.

The report also derives the Wick identity: at distinct modes, squared one-body
correlations equal minus the connected density correlation, with connected
subtraction inside each Gaussian trajectory. The saved cell estimator sums
orbitals and averages y with normalization 1/(2 Ny); coincident cells require
the contact term. It is not the connected correlator of the mixed ensemble.

Separate wall-column tail fits are in `../wall_only_tail_windows_v1/` and are
deliberately excluded from the report. No new simulations or raw-data edits.
