**Power-law scaling of the hard-wall correlator.** For a chiral complex fermion,
the Green function falls as $d^{-1}$ and the squared correlator therefore has the
critical prediction $C_G\propto d^{-2}$. (a) The $x$-averaged correlator for six
circumferences, with the $d^{-2}$ guide shown dashed. (b) Trajectory-resolved
exponents transferred against $1/N_y$; the solid and dashed curves use linear
$1/N_y$ and $1/N_y^2$ corrections, and the horizontal line marks $\beta=2$.
Each size contains $S=100$ independent pure half-filled trajectories at
$N_x=20$, evolved with hard-wall perfect correction to $t=2N_y$. Fits use
$2\le r_y\le\lfloor N_y/4\rfloor$ in the cylinder chord distance. Bars show the
trajectory interquartile range, not an error on the mean.
