\begin{abstract}
This document turns the proposed wall-charge pump into an implementation-ready
experiment and records the exact flattened-Hamiltonian benchmark that motivates
it.  In the exact $20\times40$ reference, overlap-continued wall branches have
signed spectral flow $(-1,+1)$ at $x=5,15$ and transfer one particle between
wall basins to finite-size accuracy.  Strict instantaneous ground-state
occupation instead has zero net closed-cycle transfer.  The apparent $0.98$
versus $1$ directional asymmetry is an endpoint convention at a
$10^{-9}$-scale physical-wall avoided crossing; a genuine reverse traversal
undoes the pump to numerical precision.  The earlier half-$y$
entanglement-flow estimator fails its robustness gate and is not used.

For the monitored circuit, raw wall-charge change is not a conserved-current
observable because feedback ancillas inject and remove particles.  The required
observable is a source-subtracted regional balance built from live charge and
recorded gain/loss.  We specify the flux schedule, canonical GPU API extension,
observers, output schema, controls, tests, staged campaign, and claim ladder.
The online Born-sampled pump is kept distinct from both frozen-record static
replay and H1 modular-Hamiltonian propagation.  This note is intended for
direct handoff to an implementation agent.
\end{abstract}

\section{Conventions and formalism}
\label{sec:formalism}

\subsection{Correlation matrix and trajectory}

Following BPJ, the physical correlation matrix is
\begin{equation}
  G_{ij}=\Tr(\hat\rho\,\hat\psi_i^\dagger\hat\psi_j).
  \label{eq:G}
\end{equation}
The GPU source calls its centered matrix \code{G}; here that code-level object
is
\begin{equation}
  \Geng=2G-\mathbf{1},\qquad G=(\mathbf{1}+\Geng)/2.
  \label{eq:centered}
\end{equation}
The symbol $\tr$ denotes a single-particle trace and $\Tr$ a many-body trace.
A pure number-conserving Gaussian state has $G^2=G$.

A record $\bm m=(m_1,\ldots,m_D)$ produces the measurement evolution operator
(mEO) $\hat V_{\bm m}=\hat K_{m_D}\cdots\hat K_{m_1}$.  In the online protocol,
every $m_e$ is drawn from its instantaneous Born probability.  No word is
imposed.

\subsection{Flux threading}

Thread a $y$-cycle holonomy by
\begin{equation}
 \hat c_{x,y+N_y,\mu}=e^{\ii\phi}\hat c_{x,y,\mu}.
 \label{eq:bc}
\end{equation}
The CPU exact construction uses
\begin{equation}
 k_y(m,\phi)=2\pi m/N_y+\phi/N_y,
 \label{eq:ky}
\end{equation}
and closes by
\begin{equation}
 H(\phi+2\pi)=U_yH(\phi)U_y^\dagger,\quad
 [U_y]_{xy\mu,xy\mu}=e^{\ii2\pi y/N_y}.
 \label{eq:closure}
\end{equation}

GPU production uses its existing seam-gauge controller twist.  A component
acquires $e^{\ii\phi w}$ when its center-to-component path winds
$w\in\{-1,0,+1\}$ through the seam.  Real-space diagonal regions and
$\tr(R_aP_e)$ are then flux independent.  Reject a time-dependent uniform
gauge until the associated temporal frame kick exists.

Increasing $\phi$ creates an azimuthal electromotive force; a Chern response is
transverse and moves charge between the $x$-separated walls, as in Laughlin's
Corbino argument~\cite{Laughlin1981,Halperin1982}.  The real-space measurement
does not require good $k_y$.  Twisted boundaries remain the standard
disorder/interacting formulation~\cite{NiuThouless1984,NiuThoulessWu1985}.

\subsection{Wall charge and chirality}

Let $R_L+R_R=\mathbf{1}$.  The exact calculation uses nearest-wall Voronoi
basins at $x_L=5,x_R=15$, with ties split equally.  The circuit uses
\begin{equation}
 R_L:x=0,\ldots,9,\qquad R_R:x=10,\ldots,19.
 \label{eq:regions}
\end{equation}
For slab $x\in[5,15]$, the physical interface bonds are $(4,5)$ and $(15,16)$;
the regional cuts lie in the bulk.

For a number-conserving exact state,
\begin{align}
 Q_a(\phi)&=\tr[R_aG(\phi)],\quad a=L,R,\\
 q_{\rm pump}&=(\Delta Q_R-\Delta Q_L)/2.
 \label{eq:qpump}
\end{align}
Positive sign transfers charge from $x=5$ to $x=15$ under increasing twist.
For a reference wall branch,
\begin{equation}
 \partial_\phi E_w=N_y^{-1}\partial_{k_y}E_w=v_{y,w}/N_y.
 \label{eq:velocity}
\end{equation}
The increasing-twist benchmark has
\begin{equation}
 (\cF_{x=5},\cF_{x=15})=(-1,+1),
 \label{eq:flowsign}
\end{equation}
so $v_y<0$ at $x=5$ and $v_y>0$ at $x=15$; reversal flips both.
Counterpropagating nonchiral pairs cancel in signed flow.  This is direct
chirality evidence, while charge transfer is its integrated real-space
response~\cite{Hatsugai1993,Yasuda2017}.

\subsection{Purity}

Regional charge is defined for pure or mixed $G$ and is linear:
\begin{equation}
 \tr(R_a\overline G)=\overline{\tr(R_aG)}.
\end{equation}
Retain the trajectory distribution even when computing the mean from
$\overline G$.  Ordinary pumped charge is not generically integer for arbitrary
mixed occupations.  A purity gap can protect a mixed-state invariant without
quantizing this linear charge~\cite{BudichDiehl2015,Bardyn2018}; quantized
open-system pumps require extra structure~\cite{Linzner2016}.

\subsection{Two state prescriptions}

The instantaneous prescription reoccupies the globally lowest 800 eigenstates
at every $\phi$.  It is the strict finite-size ground-state projector, closes
under Eq.~\eqref{eq:closure}, and has zero \emph{net endpoint} pump.  Its
intermediate regional charge is not constant.

The continued prescription chooses occupations once at $\phi_0$.  In each
$k_y$ block it uses Hungarian one-to-one assignment to maximize
\begin{equation}
 \sum_j|\langle u_j(\phi_i)|u_{\pi(j)}(\phi_{i+1})\rangle|^2.
\end{equation}
Within clusters $|E_i-E_j|<10^{-9}$, an SVD polar rotation aligns the new and
old subspaces.  Occupations are never reselected.  This is wall-localized
diabatic continuation through the exponentially small wall anticrossing while
remaining bulk-adiabatic.  It is neither finite-time Schr\"odinger evolution
nor the strict instantaneous ground state.

\subsection{Validation and result}

Errors are: Hermiticity $0$; $k_y$ off-block $<9.5\times10^{-16}$; block
reconstruction $<7.1\times10^{-15}$; projector idempotency
$<2.3\times10^{-15}$; rank $<2.3\times10^{-13}$; uniform/seam spectrum
$<7.4\times10^{-14}$; uniform/seam entanglement spectrum
$<1.3\times10^{-14}$; and Hamiltonian $2\pi$ closure
$<7.3\times10^{-15}$.  Instantaneous projector closure is
$<1.2\times10^{-9}$ under its explicit $5\times10^{-9}$ tolerance.
Continued monodromy is $0.05=\sqrt{2/800}$, one particle--hole replacement.

The continued wall branches have one signed flow per wall and no ambiguous
crossings on 33/65/129 meshes.  Because the strict finite spectrum anticrosses,
Eq.~\eqref{eq:flowsign} is the diabatic wall branch, not a literal
instantaneous zero.

\subsection{Endpoint artifact and reversal}

The $10^{-7}$ start offset avoids a near-degenerate wall pair; it is not a
Hamiltonian regulator.  At $\phi=0$, closest levels are
\begin{equation}
 E=\pm5.49020\times10^{-10}\ ({\rm untruncated}),\qquad
 E=\pm7.51109\times10^{-10}\ ({\rm truncated}).
\end{equation}
At $+10^{-7}$, the occupied near-zero state's left/right weights are
$(0.991985,0.008014)$ and $(0.990404,0.009595)$, explaining $0.983972$ and
$0.980810$.

Changing offset sign swaps which independent winding appears integer.  At
$N_y=40$ without truncation,
\begin{align}
 \phi_0=-10^{-7}:&\quad q_+=0.9999999976,\quad q_-=-0.9839718413,\\
 \phi_0=+10^{-7}:&\quad q_+=0.9839718590,\quad q_-=-0.9999999924.
\end{align}
The truncated case behaves identically with $0.98080969$.  Independent
opposite windings from the same initial projector are not time reverses.

A true undo starts from the forward endpoint and traverses the cached path
backward.  Across $N_y=16,24,40$, both offsets and truncations,
$q_{\rm undo}=-q_{\rm forward}$ to stored precision; return error
$\|G_{\rm return}-G_0\|_F/(2N_xN_y)$ is
$5.3\times10^{-22}$--$4.2\times10^{-18}$.  This validates tracking, not
chirality.  Future loops should put the crossing inside the path: use primary
$\phi_\star=\pi$, with $0$ as an origin control.  That origin has not yet been
run in the saved exact artifact.

\subsection{Why H3 was retired}

The half-$y$ cut creates two entanglement endpoints on each of two walls.  The
audit varied 33/65/129/refined grids, 16/24/32/48 modes, five reference levels,
wall/endpoint widths 1/2/3, seam, direction, state prescription, and full-$y$
cuts.  Physical wall flow passed, but entanglement flow did not.  Saved
classifications are grid/sensitivity plus tracking ambiguity (instantaneous)
and grid/sensitivity plus cut mismatch (continued); all four cases have
\code{genuine\_flattened\_state=False}.  This rejects the estimator, not wall
chirality.  Direct wall charge replaces it.

\section{Monitored-channel pump}
\label{sec:circuit}

\subsection{Operational distinction}

The primary experiment is one online Born-sampled ramp: equilibrate and then
continue the same trajectory while changing twist once per complete Markov
cycle.  A fixed-record replay instead imposes one word in independent static
reruns and is only a conditional response.  H1 evolves in modular time and is
an independent propagation test.  These three outputs must never share a
single pump label.

\subsection{Source-subtracted balance}

If event $e$ measures $m_e\in\{0,1\}$ and targets $s_e\in\{0,1\}$, feedback
transfers
\begin{equation}
 \delta n_e=s_e-m_e.
\end{equation}
Raw regional charge therefore mixes redistribution and injection.  Define
\begin{align}
 N_a(c)&=\tr[R_aG(c)]=\tfrac12\tr[R_a(\mathbf{1}+\Geng(c))],\\
 A_a(c)&=\sum_{c_0<e\le c}(s_e-m_e)\tr(R_aP_e),\\
 q_a(c)&=N_a(c)-N_a(c_0)-A_a(c),\\
 q_x(c)&=[q_R(c)-q_L(c)]/2.
 \label{eq:circuitpump}
\end{align}
Here $P_e=|\chi_e\rangle\langle\chi_e|$ is normalized and $c_0$ is the last
equilibration cycle.  Require
\begin{equation}
 \Delta N_{\rm total}=\sum_e(s_e-m_e),\qquad q_L+q_R=0
 \label{eq:continuity}
\end{equation}
to numerical tolerance.

This is a generalized monitored-channel pump, not literal electrical current:
conditioned measurement innovation remains.  A stronger electrical claim
requires compiling every OW measurement into a fixed local Givens tree,
performing the number measurement and filled/empty ancilla fermionic SWAP,
uncomputing the tree, and counting charge across a spatial cut gate by gate.
Validate two trees and two cut placements.

\subsection{Deterministic mean screen}

For normalized $P$, $Q=\mathbf{1}-P$, and target $s$, perfect correction gives
\begin{equation}
 \overline G'=Q\overline GQ+sP,\qquad
 \overline{\delta n}=s-\tr(P\overline G).
 \label{eq:mean}
\end{equation}
\code{mean\_replacement=True} implements the first but does not yet emit the
second.  Add that source observer and run the deterministic screen before
physical trajectories.  It is not a substitute for their distribution.

\section{Ramp and campaign arms}
\label{sec:ramp}

Let $T_{\rm eq}=2N_y$ and $T_\phi$ be an \emph{additional} pump duration.  For
orientation $\sigma=\pm1$,
\begin{equation}
 f(u)=u-\frac{\sin(2\pi u)}{2\pi},\qquad f'(0)=f'(1)=0,
\end{equation}
and define an absolute schedule of length $T_{\rm eq}+T_\phi+1$:
\begin{equation}
 \phi_c=\begin{cases}
 \phi_\star,&0\le c\le T_{\rm eq},\\
 \phi_\star+\sigma2\pi f[(c-T_{\rm eq})/T_\phi],
 &T_{\rm eq}<c\le T_{\rm eq}+T_\phi.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:schedule}
\end{equation}
Use primary $\phi_\star=\pi$ and repeat the pilot at $0$.  A hold arm remains
at $\phi_\star$.  Apply $\phi_c$ before the first update of cycle $c$; baseline
at $c_0=T_{\rm eq}$ without reinitialization.  Use fresh random schedules and
instantaneous Born outcomes.

Fix $n_{\rm shell}=1$, $\alpha_{\rm top}=1$, $\alpha_{\rm triv}=30$, trial
$X$, pure half filling, perfect correction, complex128, random order, and no
postselection.  Compare:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item soft explicit interface: \code{dw\_truncation=False},
 \code{meas\_slab\_only=False};
 \item hard support termination: both flags \code{True};
 \item matched trivial: $\alpha_{\rm top}=\alpha_{\rm triv}=30$;
 \item $+2\pi$, $-2\pi$, and hold.
\end{enumerate}
Record \code{DW\_loc}.  GPU default gives $[5,15]$ for $N_x=20$, CPU default
gives $[4,16]$; CPU parity must pass \code{dw\_interval=(5,15)}.

Use
\begin{equation}
 C_{\rm odd}(T_\phi)=\frac{\varsigma}{2}
 [\overline{q_x(+2\pi)}-\overline{q_x(-2\pi)}],
\end{equation}
with geometry sign $\varsigma$ fixed from the exact reference.  Retain even and
hold responses.  Fit $C_{\rm odd}=C_\infty+a/T_\phi$ and test
$b/T_\phi^2$ rather than assuming the asymptote.

\section{Canonical implementation contract}
\label{sec:api}

\subsection{GPU API}

The GPU class already has the absolute setter
\begin{verbatim}
set_controller_twist(phi, gauge="seam", seam_y=None)
\end{verbatim}
but no schedule.  Add to
\path{src/fgtn/classA_U1FGTN_gpu.py}:
\begin{verbatim}
controller_twist_schedule=None
controller_twist_gauge="seam"
controller_twist_seam_y=None
mean_transfer_observer=None
\end{verbatim}
with this contract:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item Schedule is \code{None} or a finite 1D array of absolute unwrapped
 fluxes of length \code{cycles + 1}; reject all else.
 \item Reset to \code{schedule[0]} before cycle-zero observation in every batch.
 Calls are absolute, not cumulative.
 \item Apply \code{schedule[c]} before any site update in cycle $c$ and hold it
 for that complete cycle.
 \item Permit dynamic schedules only in seam gauge until the uniform-gauge
 temporal kick exists.
 \item Store schedule or cryptographic hash, gauge, seam, protocol version, and
 \code{DW\_loc} in cache identity, configuration, checkpoints, and manifest.
 Static twist must also enter cache identity.
 \item Resume only on an exact schedule-hash match; preserve old behavior
 bit-for-bit when the schedule is \code{None}.
\end{enumerate}

A callback can set the next cycle in a one-batch pilot, but production must not
use it: batching, resume, random streams, and metadata are fragile.  Do not call
the engine one cycle at a time.  The setter currently rebuilds the full local
cache at every call.  Benchmark this and optimize only with fresh-rebuild
parity.

\subsection{CPU/GPU distinction and observers}

CPU \code{construct\_OW\_projectors(..., twist\_y=phi)} reconstructs from the
shifted momentum grid.  GPU seam-dresses an existing finite-support controller.
These are distinct finite-support protocols.  Use GPU seam twist for production
and CPU uniform reconstruction for exact references and explicit tests.

For pure occupied-frame trajectories, compute $N_L,N_R$ from squared live
frame-row norms in \code{native\_cycle\_observer}; do not materialize dense
covariances each cycle.  For mean/covariance runs use Eq.~\eqref{eq:centered}.

Existing \code{record\_observer} emits
\code{transfer = target\_occupied - outcome\_occupied}.  Precompute
$w_{a,e}=\tr(R_aP_e)$ by site/channel in seam gauge.  Add
\code{mean\_transfer\_observer} emitting
\begin{verbatim}
cycle, update_index, site_id, channel_label, sample_index,
target_occupation, occupation_probability,
expected_transfer = target_occupation - occupation_probability
\end{verbatim}
per event.  Public \code{mean\_replacement\_channels(...)} supplies ordered
targets and spinors for regional weights.

\section{Tests and acceptance gates}
\label{sec:tests}

Automate before physics runs:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item schedule shape/finite validation and exact cycle-timing spy;
 \item per-batch reset and absolute/idempotent setter;
 \item constant-schedule/static-setter parity and $\phi=0$ parity;
 \item seam $2\pi$ closure, seam relocation, unchanged norms/supports/weights;
 \item local-cache update versus fresh rebuild;
 \item local/dense and covariance/physical-frame parity;
 \item rejection of dynamic uniform gauge;
 \item per-trajectory Eq.~\eqref{eq:continuity};
 \item mean replacement versus explicit Born enumeration;
 \item schedule hash in cache/save/resume; and
 \item explicit geometry parity without claiming CPU/GPU twist identity.
\end{enumerate}

Do not claim a plateau unless every trajectory passes source continuity,
$+/-$ odd response is stable, hold/even are small or modeled, matched trivial
is null, signs reverse, results survive rate/seam/region/origin controls,
density change is wall localized, soft and hard walls are reported separately,
and means include trajectory distributions and confidence intervals.  Do not
impose an integer before rate, size, gauge, origin, region, wall-protocol, and
purity systematics.

\section{Claim ladder}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item \emph{Exact reference:} opposite signed flattened-$H$ wall flow and
 nearly one fixed-label transfer, with endpoint convention stated.
 \item \emph{Mean monitored channel:} nonzero orientation-odd source-subtracted
 balance under the exact affine mean map.
 \item \emph{Physical trajectories:} stable ordinary-Born mean and quantified
 distribution under the online ramp.
 \item \emph{Chirality synthesis:} orientation agreement among pump, H1/H2
 propagation, and exact wall flow; no circuit $k_y$ is required.
 \item \emph{Electrical Hall response:} only after local gate compilation.
 \item \emph{Quantization:} only after rate, size, gauge, origin, region,
 purity, and concentration studies.
\end{enumerate}
The exact reference alone is not monitored-trajectory evidence, and true undo
alone proves only numerical reversibility.  Retired H3 is not corroboration.

\section{Related work}

The closest precedent is Juli\`a-Farr\'e \emph{et al.}, who thread flux through
an inhomogeneous Chern cylinder and infer domain Chern numbers from
left/center/right charge~\cite{JuliaFarre2024}.  Their DMRG stripe differs from
this circuit, but the regional accounting is directly analogous.

Foundations are Laughlin, TKNN, Thouless, and twisted-boundary disorder
extensions~\cite{Laughlin1981,TKNN1982,Thouless1983,NiuThouless1984,NiuThoulessWu1985}.
Hatsugai and Halperin connect invariants to edge flow
~\cite{Hatsugai1993,Halperin1982}.  Mixed/open caveats are in
Refs.~\cite{BudichDiehl2015,Bardyn2018,Linzner2016,Avron2012}.
Experiments include ultracold fermion/boson pumps
~\cite{Nakajima2016,Lohse2016}, disorder-dependent pumping
~\cite{Nakajima2021}, an atomic Hall cylinder~\cite{Fabre2022}, and a quantum
anomalous Hall Corbino pump~\cite{Kawamura2023}; see also the review
~\cite{CitroAidelsburger2023}.  To our knowledge, prior work does not combine
adaptive Born trajectories, wall-resolved source subtraction, and a
flattened-$H$ comparison.  Any novelty statement must retain that qualifier.
