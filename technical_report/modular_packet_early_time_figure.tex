    Hard-wall $20\times32$ endpoints with $n_{\rm shell}=1$, $\alpha_2=30$,
    and $S=100$ independent trajectories per $\alpha_1$. Random pure states
    with Born-conditioned exterior product preparation undergo 64 raster-ordered
    cycles with perfect correction in complex128. Each of 32 translated cuts
    retains all $x$ columns and 16 consecutive $y$ rows. Independent charge-2
    packets occupy both orbitals at $(x,y_{\rm rel})=(5,8),(15,8)$.
    (a,b) For $\alpha_1=1$, evolved density is summed within periodic $x$
    distance 2 of each source wall and normalized by instantaneous retained
    charge, separately for every trajectory and cut. Conditional probabilities
    are averaged over cuts within each trajectory and then trajectories.
    Maps share a linear probability scale. Position $y_{\rm rel}-8$ is relative
    to injection, not the cut origin; dashed guides mark zero. Colored curves
    give the maps' first moments. (c) Mean displacement from injection for both
    ensembles: orange/blue denote $\alpha_1=1$, gray/black the $\alpha_1=3$
    control, solid/dashed the $x=5,15$ sources. (d) The wall contrast
    $D_\chi=(\Delta y_5-\Delta y_{15})/2$ is formed within each trajectory
    before averaging. Bands are $\pm$ one trajectory SEM; cuts are not
    independent samples, and the ensembles are not paired. Evolution uses
    $\exp(-iK_A t_{\rm mod})$, with
    $K_A=-2\operatorname{arctanh}(2C_A-\mathbf{1}_A)$ and centered eigenvalues
    clipped to $[-1+10^{-10},1-10^{-10}]$. Signs persist across the tested
    cutoffs, while magnitudes and ripple periods are cutoff dependent.
    The control is weakly mobile, not exactly stationary; $D_\chi$ is not a
    topological invariant. Curves span $0\leq t_{\rm mod}\leq1$ at spacing
    $0.01$, without smoothing, unwrapping, or velocity fits. Modular time is
